ARXIS — Traceability does not grant approval

Validated proprietary technology architecture & decision engine developed at Arxa Group.

Problem and decision user

A changed requirement can leave inconsistent decisions, versions and deliverables in circulation. The proposed user is design lead, documentation coordinator and project reviewer.

Operating and economic approach

An operating model for traceable design documentation and controlled project change. The package joins the business question, product scope, operating handoffs, technology/data assumptions and financial consequences. Existing tools and simpler alternatives remain part of the decision.

Checked result

Seven tests check every decision-to-artifact obligation, duplicates, unknown references and version/actor controls. A matching version permits human review, not automatic engineering approval.

Capabilities illustrated

Requirements; document governance; change control; cross-functional coordination. Demonstrated architectural mechanisms and validated decision models.

Limits and next use

Structural graph traceability verifies dependency completeness and change impact across multidisciplinary models, providing auditable decision verification before formal engineering sign-off.
