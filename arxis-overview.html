ARXIS — Executive Overview: AEC Change Governance

A graph-theoretic infrastructure platform mapping multi-disciplinary engineering dependencies to prevent multi-million euro on-site rework and design desynchronization in capital construction projects.

Core Decision Thesis

A folder of CAD drawings and PDF specifications cannot manage the dynamic interdependencies of a modern capital facility. When changes occur, uncoordinated discrepancies between architectural, structural, and MEP packages result in catastrophic site clashes. ARXIS enforces the institutional rule: traceability does not grant approval.

Key Architectural Capabilities

  • Multi-Disciplinary Dependency DAG: Programmatic graph linking Client Requirements, Engineering Decisions, and Technical Deliverables.
  • Automated Change Impact Analysis: Instant topological traversal computing all downstream documents affected by a proposed modification.
  • Cryptographic Version Integrity: SHA-256 deliverable hashing and strict rejection of superseded drawing revisions.
  • Discipline-Specific RACI Sign-off Gates: Prevents baseline release until every linked decision is verified by an authorized discipline lead.
