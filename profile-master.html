Management of capital, operations and technology

Private profile draft, updated 2026-10-26. Based on canonical career records and the current management portfolio; not a change to the CV.

Professional narrative

I combine private-capital operations, corporate banking analysis and technology-project development with structured business, product and operating models.

The portfolio demonstrates documented decisions, assumptions, operating ownership and financial/technology trade-offs. It is not positioned as five production software businesses.

Multidimensional positioning

The intended specialization is the interface between capital decisions, quantitative risk, technology products and operating systems. Technical specificity remains part of the proposition: statistics and probabilistic models, data architecture and engineering, AI/retrieval, transaction states and infrastructure documentation. Management supplies the accountability, economics and adoption process that allow these strands to be used together.

The development thesis is to preserve several technically informed options and progressively test their value in private-capital, infrastructure and data/product contexts. It is not a claim of proven investment outperformance or an independently established unique market premium.
