Hobbly — Executive Overview: Transaction State Machine

A deterministic transaction orchestration engine governing multi-party reservations, payment pre-authorizations, and automated dispute escrow to safeguard unit contribution margins.

Core Decision Thesis

A marketplace reservation is not completed by a payment authorization alone; it requires continuous agreement between inventory locks, customer rights, host fulfillment, and payment settlement. Hobbly enforces the fundamental operational law: exceptions determine the operating model.

Key Architectural Capabilities

  • Deterministic Finite State Automaton (FSM): Enforces valid state transitions across the full booking, cancellation, and refund lifecycle.
  • Cryptographic Request Idempotency: SHA-256 fingerprinting prevents double-charging and duplicate refund processing.
  • Automated Escrow & Dispute Reconciliation: Dual-entry accounting tracking Gross Captured vs. Gross Refunded amounts with zero leakage.
  • Contribution Margin Safeguards: Self-service cancellation workflows engineered to keep support friction strictly below the €3.50 breakeven ceiling.
