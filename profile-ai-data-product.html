Systems Architecture and Data Products

Designing deterministic software systems, enterprise knowledge graphs, and high-assurance data platforms for complex, regulated operational environments.

1. Specialization Overview

Building enterprise technology products requires more than writing software code; it demands a comprehensive understanding of organizational information architecture, data lineage, edge-case failure modes, and long-term operating economics. In regulated corporate landscapes, unverified black-box algorithms and undocumented data pipelines introduce massive operational vulnerabilities.

My work focuses on architecting deterministic, auditable systems where data flow, state transitions, and business decisions are mathematically verified, version-controlled, and anchored in institutional compliance frameworks.

3. Governed AI & High-Assurance RAG Systems

As enterprises adopt machine learning and Generative AI, governance becomes the defining bottleneck. Language models cannot be deployed in corporate banking, pharmaceutical trials, or legal compliance without verifiable proof that outputs are grounded in approved institutional evidence.

Through EDNA, I have modeled governed knowledge retrieval architectures that decouple candidate discovery from deterministic citation verification. By demonstrating automated contradiction quarantine and explicit abstention triggers, this prototype demonstrates technical mechanisms aligned with transparency objectives under the EU AI Act (High-Risk AI Systems) and FDA 21 CFR Part 11.

4. Independent Systems Modeling & Reference Architectures

As an independent systems modeler and quantitative researcher, I have authored four algorithmic reference architectures and reproducible prototypes:

  • ARXA (Industrial Telemetry & Real-Options Asset Valuation): Applied R&D platform bridging industrial sensor telemetry with financial engineering; models equipment degradation curves (vibration, thermal loads) and integrates Columbia-grounded binomial real-options lattices to evaluate CapEx vs. OpEx maintenance timing and flexible capital allocation under uncertainty.
  • ARXIS (Engineering Dependency Traceability & Change Governance): Systems modeling framework for multi-disciplinary engineering projects; utilizes Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAG) and critical-path analysis aligned with IBM Systems Analysis principles to track design dependencies, quantify change-propagation risk, and establish auditable change-order review workflows.
  • EDNA (Governed Knowledge Architecture & Provenance Verification): High-assurance knowledge retrieval architecture designed for regulated environments; implements cryptographic SHA-256 chunk-level provenance, citation validation, and contradiction-detection rules aligned with EU AI Act and FDA 21 CFR Part 11 audit standards to mitigate hallucination risk in document analysis.
  • Hobbly (Transaction State Machine & Settlement Economics): Commercial services architecture modeling multi-party booking and settlement workflows; engineers deterministic finite state machines (FSM) with reservation holds, escrow logic, and idempotency controls under European PSD2/SCA standards, evaluating contribution margins and payment exception handling.
