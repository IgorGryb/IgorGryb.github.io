Systems Architecture and Data Products

Designing deterministic software systems, enterprise knowledge graphs, and high-assurance data platforms for complex, regulated operational environments.

1. Specialization Overview

Building enterprise technology products requires more than writing software code; it demands a comprehensive understanding of organizational information architecture, data lineage, edge-case failure modes, and long-term operating economics. In regulated corporate landscapes, unverified black-box algorithms and undocumented data pipelines introduce massive operational vulnerabilities.

My work focuses on architecting deterministic, auditable systems where data flow, state transitions, and business decisions are mathematically verified, version-controlled, and anchored in institutional compliance frameworks.

3. Governed AI & High-Assurance RAG Systems

As enterprises adopt machine learning and Generative AI, governance becomes the defining bottleneck. Language models cannot be deployed in corporate banking, pharmaceutical trials, or legal compliance without verifiable proof that outputs are grounded in approved institutional evidence.

Through EDNA, I have pioneered governed Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures that separate multi-mode retrieval from programmatic citation entailment. By enforcing automated contradiction detection and explicit abstention triggers, this architecture eliminates hallucination risk and satisfies the strict regulatory requirements of the EU AI Act (High-Risk AI Systems) and FDA 21 CFR Part 11.

4. Arxa Group Venture Engineering

At Arxa Group, I have led the conceptualization, system modeling, and technical implementation of four production-grade reference architectures:

  • EDNA: Enterprise Knowledge Infrastructure with SHA-256 chunk lineage and deterministic contradiction detection.
  • ARXA: Industrial Telemetry & Real-Options Operating Engine connecting IoT vibration/thermal sensors with Columbia-modeled binomial lattices.
  • ARXIS: AEC Architecture Traceability & Change Governance mapping cross-disciplinary CAD/BIM dependencies into Directed Acyclic Graphs.
  • Hobbly: Transaction State Machine & Services Platform governing multi-party reservations, idempotent payments, and automated escrow reconciliations.
