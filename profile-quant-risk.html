Corporate Banking, Debt Structuring and Quantitative Risk

Underwriting corporate debt facilities, structuring syndicated credit solutions, and quantitative risk modeling for Poland's largest enterprise balance sheets.

1. Specialization Overview

Institutional debt capital allocation demands deep financial modeling, structural covenant design, and continuous sensitivity analysis under macroeconomic stress. Corporate credit decisions cannot rely on simple accounting multiples; they require cash-flow simulations under changing interest-rate regimes, operational supply-chain shocks, and evolving capital expenditure commitments.

My career bridges tier-one corporate banking coverage with rigorous mathematical and econometric training, transforming financial statements into robust, risk-adjusted decision frameworks.

2. Santander CIB Track Record

At Santander Corporate & Investment Banking (CIB), I served within the Large Corporates coverage team, managing banking relationships for Poland's largest strategic enterprises (client universe requiring a minimum annual turnover of PLN 1.2 Billion+):

  • Debt Underwriting & Credit Proposals: Drafting credit applications and financial risk memos for multi-million zloty credit facilities, working capital programs, and bilateral credit lines.
  • Syndicated Loan Modeling: Participating in consortium debt modeling, evaluating intercreditor term sheets, debt service coverage ratios (DSCR), and leverage covenants (Net Debt / EBITDA).
  • Continuous Portfolio Monitoring: Conducting quarterly financial reviews, assessing compliance with financial and non-financial covenants, and modeling liquidity buffers under adverse interest rate scenarios.

3. Debt Structuring & Underwriting Framework

Institutional debt underwriting synthesizes five core analytical pillars:

Underwriting Pillar Analytical Focus Quantitative Metrics & Stress Tests
Cash Flow Capacity Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF) generation DSCR, Interest Coverage Ratio (ICR), Cash Conversion Cycle.
Leverage & Solvency Total debt capacity and repayment feasibility Net Debt / EBITDA, Debt / Equity, Tangible Net Worth tests.
Collateral & Security Recovery value under default liquidation Loan-to-Value (LTV), asset pledges, receivables assignment haircuts.
Covenant Design Early warning tripwires and equity cure limits Maintenance vs. Incurrence covenants, restricted payment clauses.
Macro Sensitivity Resilience to FX fluctuations and rate hikes Monte Carlo rate simulations, margin compression scenarios.

4. Quantitative Methods & Stochastic Modeling

My academic specialization at SGH Warsaw School of Economics (MIESI) and graduate coursework with Columbia University provide the mathematical foundation for advanced risk engineering:

  • Stochastic Calculus & Asset Pricing: Binomial tree algorithms, geometric Brownian motion, Black-Scholes PDE, and real-options pricing under volatility.
  • Econometric Forecasting: Time-series cointegration (VECM), Vector Autoregression (VAR), GARCH volatility modeling, and panel data analysis.
  • Machine Learning for Risk: Supervised classification for default prediction (logistic regression, gradient-boosted trees), survival analysis, and stress-testing simulations.
