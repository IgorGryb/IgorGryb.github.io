Private Capital and Infrastructure Operations

Institutional custody administration, asset inventory verification, and operational governance for private equity, real estate, and infrastructure funds under AIFMD compliance.

1. Specialization Overview

Managing private capital requires unwavering operational integrity. In alternative asset classes—private equity, private debt, infrastructure, and commercial real estate—value is created not only through asset selection, but through the rigorous operational infrastructure that safeguards capital commitments, verifies physical asset ownership, and reconciles multi-currency cash flows.

My background connects corporate and institutional banking fund custody operations with modern data systems architecture, ensuring that investment sponsors and institutional limited partners maintain transparent, auditable control over their alternative asset portfolios.

2. BNP Paribas CIB Track Record

At BNP Paribas Corporate & Institutional Banking (CIB), I operated within the Alternative Investments and Custody Operations division, supporting fund administration across an international client ecosystem with over $150B in collective platform assets under custody:

  • Asset Inventory & Title Verification: Conducting independent due diligence and legal document reconciliation to confirm beneficial ownership of illiquid underlying assets (private equity shareholdings, infrastructure concessions, real estate titles).
  • Cash Flow & Liquidity Reconciliation: Tracking capital calls, distribution waterfalls, subscription receipts, and operating expense disbursements with zero unreconciled ledger discrepancies.
  • AIFMD Regulatory Compliance: Performing statutory depository oversight in strict alignment with the European Union's Alternative Investment Fund Managers Directive (AIFMD), mitigating operational risks and reporting breaches.

4. Technology & Infrastructure Systems Synergy

My fund operations background directly informs my computational decision models and algorithmic reference architectures:

  • ARXA (Industrial Real-Options Engine): Translates asset condition monitoring into quantitative capital expenditure timing, enabling private infrastructure funds to optimize maintenance reserves under uncertainty.
  • ARXIS (AEC Dependency Traceability): Provides real estate and infrastructure development sponsors with complete documentation traceability, protecting capital investments from contractor rework and cost overruns.
