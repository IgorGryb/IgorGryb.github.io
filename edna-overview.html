EDNA — Executive Overview: Governed RAG Infrastructure

A deterministic knowledge infrastructure platform decoupling document retrieval from evidence validation to eliminate AI hallucinations in regulated enterprise environments.

Core Decision Thesis

Generative AI platforms create severe compliance and operational liabilities when deployed in regulated corporate workflows. Fluency is not factual truth, and lexical proximity is not evidence support. EDNA enforces an institutional rule: retrieval is not evidence validation.

Key Architectural Capabilities

  • Cryptographic Data Lineage: SHA-256 chunk hashing, immutable version tracking, and parent-document context retention.
  • Deterministic Contradiction Detection: Proactive identification and quarantine of mutually exclusive regulatory rules before synthesis.
  • Programmatic Citation Entailment: Automated NLI scoring verifying that every assertion mathematically maps to source evidence.
  • Explicit Abstention Protocols: Failsafe mechanisms that refuse to answer rather than fabricate when source data is absent.
