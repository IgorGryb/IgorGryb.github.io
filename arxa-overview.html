ARXA — Executive Overview: Industrial Real-Options Engine

An operational analytics platform connecting high-frequency IoT sensor telemetry with Columbia-modeled real-options binomial lattices to optimize industrial maintenance CapEx under uncertainty.

Core Decision Thesis

A statistical prediction of mechanical failure creates zero economic value unless an authorized operator can take a timely, cost-effective, and resource-feasible maintenance action. ARXA enforces an essential principle: prediction has value only through a feasible action.

Key Architectural Capabilities

  • Sensor Condition Ingestion: Multi-channel vibration FFT spectral density, thermal dissipation rates, and RPM monitoring.
  • Weibull Remaining Useful Life (RUL): Statistical hazard rate modeling to map continuous asset degradation distributions.
  • Real-Options Decision Lattice: Cox-Ross-Rubinstein binomial valuation determining the optimal timing between immediate intervention CapEx and deferral flexibility.
  • Feasibility & Capacity Arbiter: Programmatic verification of crew capacity, maintenance lead times, and spare part inventory before dispatch.
