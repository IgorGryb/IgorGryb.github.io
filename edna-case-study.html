EDNA — Enterprise Knowledge Infrastructure & Governed RAG Lineage

A high-assurance enterprise decision platform engineered to eliminate generative AI hallucinations through cryptographic source lineage, deterministic contradiction detection, and programmatic citation entailment.

1. Executive Summary & Mission

In mission-critical corporate environments—such as clinical trials governance (FDA 21 CFR Part 11), corporate credit underwriting (Basel III), and AI regulatory compliance (EU AI Act)—traditional search and generative AI systems introduce intolerable operational risks. Language models generate fluent yet untraceable assertions, cite non-existent sources, and silently bypass conflicting institutional policies.

EDNA (Enterprise Decision & Narrative Architecture) was conceptualized and engineered as an algorithmic reference architecture to solve the fundamental problem of information provenance in regulated environments. EDNA establishes a strict rule: retrieval is not evidence validation. By decoupling multi-modal retrieval from formal semantic support verification, EDNA provides institutional analysts and regulatory stewards with mathematically verifiable knowledge graphs, immutable source lineage, and automated conflict quarantines.

2. Strategic Industry Problem & Context

Knowledge workers in investment banking, corporate law, and biopharma spend up to 40% of their working hours cross-referencing complex regulatory documentation. While standard Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) platforms promise automation, they introduce three systemic points of failure:

  • Lineage Degradation: Synthesized research summaries frequently lose the exact version, timestamp, jurisdiction, and authorization state of the underlying source clauses.
  • Silent Contradiction Blindness: Standard vector similarity models prioritize lexical proximity over semantic consistency, often aggregating mutually exclusive policies into a single misleading conclusion.
  • Audit & Compliance Liability: Regulated institutions cannot deploy black-box generative systems without an auditable chain of custody proving which human or model authorized every extracted fact.

3. System Architecture & Core Mechanics

EDNA operates on a zero-trust evidence pipeline structured across five deterministic layers:

Layer Component Architectural Mechanism Operational Function
Layer 1 Immutable Ingestion SHA-256 cryptographic chunk hashing & temporal tagging Guarantees document authenticity; isolates quarantined or superseded revisions.
Layer 2 Dual-Mode Retrieval Engine Hybrid BM25 lexical search + deterministic concept expansion Maximizes recall across domain terminology without introducing embedding drift.
Layer 3 Citation Entailment Filter Programmatic natural language inference (NLI) scoring Verifies that each generated assertion is logically entailed by the cited source chunk.
Layer 4 Contradiction Arbiter Deterministic conflict matrix & policy registry Halts automated generation and triggers explicit abstention when opposing rules collide.
Layer 5 Executive Memo Compiler Version-locked audit packager with exportable JSON/PDF manifests Delivers review-ready research dossiers with embedded source provenance.

4. 5-Dimensional Multidimensional Model

Per the Arxa Group Operating Standards, EDNA integrates five synchronized operational dimensions:

  • Technical Model: Modular Python reference core with isolated SQLite metadata catalog, pluggable embedding backends, and zero external network leakage during classification.
  • Product & Operating Workflow: Governed by a clear RACI matrix:
    1. Source Steward: Ingests and certifies master documentation, setting jurisdiction and expiration parameters.
    2. Research Analyst: Formulates structured queries against approved regulatory corpora.
    3. Compliance Reviewer: Audits flagged contradictions, ambiguous entailment scores, and system abstentions.
    4. Decision Sponsor: Receives immutable, digitally signed executive briefing memos.
  • Financial Model: Direct unit economics benchmarking the total cost per validated decision memo against traditional manual legal/analyst review.
  • Risk & Governance Model: Full adherence to international standards:
    • FDA 21 CFR Part 11: Tamper-evident audit trails and digital signatures.
    • EU AI Act (High-Risk AI Systems): Complete traceability, human-in-the-loop oversight, and rigorous benchmark logging.
    • Basel III / BCBS 239: Risk data aggregation integrity and verifiable reporting architectures.
  • Scaling & Extensibility: Designed to transition from localized compliance pods to enterprise-wide semantic knowledge meshes via open APIs.

5. Information & Governance Contract

EDNA enforces rigid data contracts across all ingested artifacts and compiled outputs:

7. Financial Model & Unit Economics

To establish investment feasibility, EDNA's economic model compares manual analyst research with the governed EDNA workflow across corporate knowledge operations:

  • Manual Baseline: Professional analyst time of 60 minutes @ €60.00/hour = €60.00 per accepted note.
  • Governed EDNA Model: Automated ingestion, dual-mode search, and entailment verification (0.05 hrs compute @ €2.00) plus 40 minutes of focused human review @ €60.00/hour = €42.00 per accepted note.
  • Net Economic Benefit: Direct 30% cost reduction (€18.00 savings per note) while eliminating regulatory compliance defect risk.
  • Operational Breakeven: The operational breakeven threshold is 33.0 minutes of review time. If expert review exceeds 33 minutes, the system recommends human escalation.

  1. Graph-RAG Integration: Connecting discrete regulatory clauses into bi-directional knowledge graphs (Neo4j / NetworkX) to model cross-jurisdictional compliance impact.
